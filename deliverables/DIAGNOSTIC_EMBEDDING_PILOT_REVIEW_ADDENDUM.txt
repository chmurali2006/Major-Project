2. MAIN RESULT — DO NOT SCALE THE UNCHANGED METHOD
Guided correction returns the fixed output on11/12 images; guide KL improves on only1/12. Every variant preserves all12 predicted classes. Original and received class metrics are all perfect on this tiny guide-TRAIN subset; there is no class-decision improvement available to demonstrate here. This does not contradict the earlier weaker500-image validation baseline; it is a different, training-selected subset.

Guided mean MSE is about8.18 times fixed, concentrated in one changed image. On ISIC_0061070 (MEL), fixed MSE .0764401754 rises to5.8180987040 under correction (about76.1 times); original MEL probability .6242184, fixed .6472858, guided .6140608. All three predict MEL correctly. This is guide-probability adjustment, not a clinical/classification rescue.

The distortion gate accepts11 no-op outputs and rejects the one nonzero correction, leaving fixed output on all12. Do not advertise11 accepted cases as11 successful useful corrections.

Query-matched random search also reduces mean guide KL, but at substantially greater distortion; it matches forward-query counts, not gradient cost or energy. Weaker QIM has lower mean distortion AND lower mean guide KL than guided here, but reduces decoding margin. Therefore this is not a matched-robustness superiority result for weaker QIM either.

5. WHAT THIS ADDS TO THE PROJECT
A medical-image numerical feasibility run now exists, with retained negative outcomes and fairer controls. The candidate does not yet demonstrate a useful diagnostic advantage. It should be shown as an evaluated experimental extension, not a successful novel medical watermark.

The distortion identity remains valid: fixed carrier values plus changes only to previously untouched non-carrier samples necessarily add squared distortion. This pilot cannot overturn that constraint. A lower-KL search on TRAIN alone does not justify a larger unchanged study.

Recommended next decision: stop unchanged-candidate scaling; obtain the independent review; prioritise the already-pending matched baseline validation. If a separately versioned carrier-flexible or other redesign is proposed, review its prior-art overlap, exact payload/margin constraints and decisive controls BEFORE further GPU work. Such a redesign is not implemented or validated in this addendum. An independent evaluator remains necessary for any future transfer-preservation claim, but is not by itself a remedy for weak current utility.

No request to rerun this notebook. **Status correction (2026-11-24):** an earlier ISIC-study-v2 run evaluated 1,000 test images; their IDs are unrecoverable from available retained evidence, and overlap with the later 500-image cohort is unknown. The later cohort is non-confirmatory development evidence; do not call it untouched or inspect it for additional outcomes. This addendum is a historical record of the pilot review, not evidence about split independence. See `deliverables/TEST_SPLIT_RECOVERY_DECISION.md`.
