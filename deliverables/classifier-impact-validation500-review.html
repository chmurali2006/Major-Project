Frozen classifier impact: reviewed validation results

500 ISIC images · 444 nonMEL / 56 MEL · 12 cases · 6,000 records · DenseNet121 epoch 3

Development evidence, not clinical safety or final-test performance. This experiment operates on original images. It does not measure watermark-induced effects or validate the diagnostic-correction candidate.

What passed review

All summary metrics and 2,000-repetition paired bootstrap intervals were recomputed from stored predictions. Clean-probability maximum difference from the training receipt: 9.5367431640625e-07.

Interpretation that the evidence supports

  • Local modifications are not necessarily visible to a global classifier. The random copy patch yields 4 flips and the local-blur surrogate 1. Neither operator is verified semantic lesion removal. This does not imply tampering is harmless or that authentication is unnecessary.
  • JPEG70 has a class-dependent effect. Melanoma sensitivity falls from 33/56 to 22/56, while overall accuracy rises from 86.4% to 89.2% (14 correct→wrong versus 28 wrong→correct). Accuracy alone would conceal the minority-class deterioration. BA falls 7.01 percentage points; its unadjusted paired interval is approximately −13.14 to −1.31 pp.
  • The frozen model is vulnerable to the adapted white-box attacks. FGSM makes 80/432 initially correct predictions wrong (18.52%); PGD makes 430/432 wrong (99.54%). Overall wrong predictions are 138/500 and 498/500 respectively. These denominators must not be confused.
  • PGD is not a universal attacker result. It uses access to this classifier's input gradients and ground-truth labels, clean-start untargeted cross-entropy ascent, RGB epsilon 4/255, 3 steps of 1/255, and integer output evaluation. No transfer/black-box or exact author attack reproduction is established.

What this means for the project

Integrity detection and classifier impact answer different questions. A content change can matter for authenticity without changing the global diagnosis prediction; a visually modest operation can affect this model's sensitivity. These observations motivate measuring both endpoints separately. They do not establish a benefit for the implemented correction, explain its historical motivation retrospectively, or resolve its additional-distortion limitation. The next extension experiment remains the bounded TRAIN pilot with six controls; independent-model evaluation is needed before transfer claims.

Limitations and exclusions

  • Stored predictions were audited, not independently rerun on the source images. Image/operator/attack output pixels were not returned for independent replay.
  • Validation selected the checkpoint. Intervals are descriptive, conditional on observed class counts, not patient-level; no multiple-comparison correction. Do not infer clinical significance or equivalence from interval overlap with zero.
  • The clean model already misses 23/56 melanomas. No calibration, human clinical review, clinical safety, novel mechanism or SOTA claim follows.
  • No test-split evaluation was run by this review. The returned summary declares test_evaluated=false and returned source matches the validation-only notebook.
